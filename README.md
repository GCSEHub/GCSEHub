# GCSEHub

A free GCSE revision app with subject-specific quizzes, starter tests and revision materials. Choose the subjects, exam boards and course options you study to tailor your revision.

## Open GCSEHub

**[Go to the live app](https://gcsehub.gcsehub-commodore293.workers.dev/)** or download offline link

## Features

- Revision across a range of GCSE subjects
- Subject, exam board and course-option selection
- Higher and Foundation options for Maths
- Topic quizzes, mixed quizzes and starter tests
- Progress saved in your browser on your device

## Getting started

Open the live app and choose your course setup. Then select a subject and start revising.

## Progress and privacy

Your progress is stored in your browser on your device. It does not automatically sync between devices. The app does not send your quiz answers or progress to the repository owner.

## About

GCSEHub is an independent, browser-based revision project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC or any other exam board. Check your exam board’s specification and official resources for the requirements of your course.

## Contributing

Found a problem or have an idea to improve GCSEHub? Open an issue in this repository with a clear description of the subject, page and steps to reproduce it.
